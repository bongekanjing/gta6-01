Vice City Nights — a top-down open-world crime game: explore a living city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the police as your wanted level rises, take on missions, and build your empire. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network requests — just static files.

## Play

- **WASD / arrows** — move on foot, drive when in a car
- **E** — enter / exit the nearest car (steal parked or traffic cars)
- **Space** — shoot / handbrake while driving
- **M** — toggle mission list, **P** — pause, **R** — restart after busted/wasted

Earn cash from missions, pickups and car deliveries. The police respond to your wanted level — lose them by breaking line of sight. Progress autosaves to your browser.
